Frequently asked questions

How does Clean Space work?

Clean Space helps you free storage by reviewing a few items at a time so it’s quick and manageable. You choose which albums to include and their order, set how many items to review per session, and pick reminder times (evenly spaced in your active hours). When a session starts, it shows a few items from the active album; you swipe left to delete or right to keep, and confirm at the end.

Do you delete anything automatically?

Never. You choose each item to delete, and nothing is removed until you confirm at the end of a session. If you don’t mark it, it stays.

Is my data safe and private?

Your media stay on your device, and we don't upload or store your photos or videos. Clean Space uses no accounts and no ad tracking. Your settings and progress are saved locally. Limited diagnostic crash data may be sent to Sentry to help improve stability, and your purchase status is checked through RevenueCat using an anonymous ID.

What is free, and what is Clean Space Unlimited?

One review session a day, of up to 5 items, is free for as long as you like. Clean Space Unlimited is a one-time purchase that removes the daily limit and allows up to 20 items per session. There is no subscription. If you bought it before, use Restore purchase on the Unlimited screen in Settings.

Will deleting photos affect my cloud backups?

Photos deleted through Clean Space are removed from your device's local storage. If you have cloud backup services enabled (like iCloud or Google Photos), the deletion behavior depends on your sync settings. We recommend checking your cloud service settings before deletions.

Notifications aren’t showing—what can I do?

Ensure notifications are allowed in system settings, disable Do Not Disturb/Focus during your active hours, and verify your schedule in Settings.
