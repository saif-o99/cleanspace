Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Clean Space is a privacy-focused app that helps you free device storage. Your media review and deletion workflow happens on your device, and the app sends only limited diagnostics needed for stability and the purchase status needed to unlock Clean Space Unlimited.

2. Data We Process (On-Device Only)

  • Photos/Media access: to display your items and delete only those you explicitly select.
  • App settings & progress: stored locally on your device (e.g., preferences, album order, counters).
  • Diagnostics (online when available): crash reports, stack traces, error messages, app version, device model/OS, and basic app state at the time of an error. No media contents are included.
  • Purchase status (online when available): an anonymous app-generated ID and your Clean Space Unlimited purchase history (product, date, store, country/region and price), so the app can unlock and restore what you bought. No name, email or payment details are collected by us.
  • Feedback you send (optional): the message you write in Report a bug, plus a name and email if you choose to enter them.

3. How We Use Data

  • Show your photos/videos for review.
  • Execute deletions only for items you select.
  • Remember your preferences (e.g., theme, items per session, schedule).
  • Unlock and restore Clean Space Unlimited if you buy it.

No automated deletion occurs.

4. Storage & Security

All photo/media processing happens on your device. App settings are stored locally using secure storage. The app sends limited diagnostic events securely to our processor (Sentry) for troubleshooting, and purchase status to our purchase processor (RevenueCat). We never transmit your media files.

5. Permissions

  • Photos/Media (required): to read and delete items you choose.
  • Notifications (optional): to schedule local reminders you configure.

6. Networking & Third Parties

The app works offline; most features require no network. We use Sentry for crash/error reporting only. Data sent is limited to diagnostics described above and is used solely to improve stability. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play; it receives the purchase status described above and no media. Payments themselves are handled by the store. Sentry and RevenueCat process this data under their own privacy policies and data protection terms.

7. Deletion & Device Behavior

On iOS, storage may not be reclaimed until the system clears Recently Deleted.

On Android, deletion is typically permanent.

Uninstalling the app removes app-stored settings and local stats.

8. Children’s Privacy

Clean Space is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the “Last updated” date. Material changes will be highlighted in-app.
